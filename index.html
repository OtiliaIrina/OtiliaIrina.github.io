About Me

I am a PhD student in the Astrophysics Research Group at UCL, supervised by Prof. Benjamin Joachimi. My research focuses on cosmology and the large-scale structure of the Universe, and I am involved in the DESI survey.

Research

My work involves observational cosmology, cosmological inference, and analysis of large-scale structure datasets, particularly within the DESI (Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument) collaboration.

Research Interests

  • Observational Cosmology
  • Large-Scale Structure of the Universe
  • DESI Survey
