Research focus

Turning a forest of absorption lines into a map of the Universe.

The Lyman-α forest traces matter at redshifts that are difficult to reach with ordinary galaxy surveys. My work focuses on the statistical machinery needed to extract that information accurately and efficiently.

01

Analytical covariance

Building a real-space covariance model for Lyman-α correlation functions from Fourier-space signal, aliasing and spectral-noise contributions.

02

2D FFTLog transforms

Using multipole decompositions and two-dimensional FFTLog machinery to propagate anisotropic power-spectrum information into correlation-function space.

03

Forecasting DESI

Studying what future Lyman-α, quasar and high-redshift tracer samples can teach us about cosmic expansion and structure growth.

Current direction

From covariance modelling to cosmological forecasts.

A reliable covariance matrix is the bridge between a theoretical signal and trustworthy parameter constraints. I am developing that bridge for the anisotropic three-dimensional Lyman-α forest.
