About

Turning cosmic structure into precise measurements.

I am a PhD researcher at University College London working in observational cosmology. My research connects theory, statistical inference, and survey data to understand how well high-redshift tracers can constrain the expansion and growth of the Universe.

A central part of my work is developing an analytical covariance model for the three-dimensional Lyman-α forest correlation function and using it in forecasting pipelines for future DESI analyses.

What I work on

01

Analytical covariance

Building a real-space covariance matrix for the Lyman-α forest from Fourier-space signal, aliasing, and noise contributions.

02

Cosmological forecasting

Translating survey design and tracer properties into expected constraints using Fisher information and realistic covariance models.

03

DESI Lyman-α analyses

Studying three-dimensional clustering, full-shape information, fiducial cosmology validation, and future high-redshift samples.
