Research

Selected research themes and ongoing work in Lyman-α forest cosmology.

Ongoing work

Analytical Lyman-α covariance

I am constructing a Gaussian analytical covariance for the anisotropic three-dimensional Lyman-α forest correlation function. The calculation begins with the measured Fourier-space power, including the cosmological signal, transverse-sampling aliasing and spectral noise, and propagates these contributions into real-space separation bins.

Covariance · Fourier space · real space

2D FFTLog and multipoles

The Lyman-α power spectrum depends on both wavenumber and angle to the line of sight. I decompose this anisotropic information into Legendre multipoles, transform the radial dependence efficiently, and reconstruct the covariance over parallel and transverse separations.

FFTLog · Legendre multipoles · correlation functions

Full-shape validation

I have also worked on fiducial-cosmology tests for Lyman-α full-shape analyses. These tests examine whether the inference pipeline remains unbiased when the cosmology used to convert observed angles and redshifts into comoving coordinates differs from the cosmology of the data.

DESI · validation · full shape

Future-survey forecasts

My forecasting work looks ahead to denser quasar samples and additional high-redshift tracers, asking which combinations of auto- and cross-correlations provide the most useful information about expansion, growth and survey design.

Fisher forecasts · quasars · future tracers
